6. OBSERVATIONS
----------------------------------------
1. Nature of IFND Dataset:
   - IFND is an Indian multimodal fake news dataset featuring statement headlines,
     associated web publisher sources (29 sources like DNAINDIA, TRIBUNEINDIA, THEPRINT),
     image URLs (46,755 unique image links), category tags, and binary labels ('TRUE'/'Fake').
   - The text statements represent concise headline-style news claims from Indian media contexts.

2. Nature of LIAR Dataset:
   - LIAR is a benchmark political claim dataset containing 12,791 statements collected from PolitiFact.
   - It uses a 6-class fine-grained truth scale: half-true, false, mostly-true, true, barely-true, pants-fire.
   - Includes rich speaker context, party affiliation, speaker state, speaker credit history counts, and venue.
   - It is entirely text-based with zero image references.

3. Multilingual and Multimodal Complementarity:
   - IFND provides multimodal Indian-context news grounding essential for FakeShield's Indian news target.
   - LIAR provides structured fine-grained claim classification and rich statement verification nuance.
   - In accordance with FakeShield specifications, IFND and LIAR remain strictly segregated and unmerged.

7. PROBLEMS FOUND & DATA QUALITY FINDINGS
----------------------------------------
1. Character Encoding in Raw IFND:
   - Problem: IFND.csv contains non-UTF-8 bytes (e.g. byte 0xd4 for curly quotes) causing UnicodeDecodeError
     when read with standard UTF-8.
   - Resolution: Loaded with Latin-1 (latin1/iso-8859-1) encoding and standardized to clean UTF-8 output.

2. High Proportion of Missing Dates in IFND:
   - Problem: 11,321 out of 56,714 rows (19.96%) in IFND have missing values for the 'Date' column.
   - Impact: Temporal grounding cannot rely solely on the Date column without fallback mechanisms.

3. Typographical Category Error in IFND:
   - Problem: 'Category' column contains a typo entry 'MISLEADIND' (18 rows) alongside 'MISLEADING' (4,059 rows).
   - Impact: When filtering or aggregating by category, 'MISLEADIND' should be recognized as 'MISLEADING'.

4. Class Imbalance in IFND:
   - Problem: IFND has 37,800 TRUE rows (66.65%) and 18,914 Fake rows (33.35%), creating a 2:1 majority for TRUE.
   - Impact: Evaluation metrics must use balanced/macro F1 rather than raw accuracy.

5. Missing Header and Metadata in LIAR:
   - Problem: Raw LIAR TSV files contain no header row, requiring explicit schema mapping.
   - In addition, metadata columns have missing values: job_title is missing in 27.9% of rows,
     state_info is missing in 21.5% of rows, and context is missing in 1.0% of rows.
   - Impact: Core statement and label columns are 100% complete; metadata sparsity must be handled gracefully.
